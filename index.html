Hyena Capital - Contractor Onboarding Portal

Important: All contractors must complete onboarding tasks within 5 business days of engagement start date. Contact Nancy Admin for any access issues.

Onboarding Checklist

  1. Sign and return NDA and contractor agreement via DocuSign
  2. Complete security awareness training (link sent via email)
  3. Set up MFA on all Hyena Capital systems
  4. Request access to project-specific SharePoint folders
  5. Review data handling and classification policy below
  6. Schedule kickoff call with project lead
  7. Submit equipment request form if applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I get access to SharePoint?
A: Submit a request to Nancy Admin with your project name and manager approval. Access is typically granted within 24 hours.

Q: What tools will I need?
A: Most contractors use Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, and project-specific tools. Your project lead will confirm the full list at kickoff.

Q: Who do I contact for IT issues?
A: Email helpdesk@hyenacapital.net or message the IT Support channel in Teams. For urgent issues, call the helpdesk at ext. 4401.

Q: What is the expense reimbursement process?
A: Submit expenses through the contractor expense portal within 30 days. Attach receipts and tag your project code. Finance processes reimbursements bi-weekly.

Q: How do I access the VPN?
A: VPN access is granted after security training is completed. Download the GlobalProtect client and use your contractor credentials.
