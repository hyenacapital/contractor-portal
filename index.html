Hyena Capital - Contractor Onboarding Portal

Welcome to the Hyena Capital contractor onboarding portal. Please follow all steps below.

Step 1: Submit Tax Documents

W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (international). Must be stored securely.

Step 2: Sign Your Contract

Details scope of work, payment terms, duration, termination policy, and IP rights.

Step 3: Sign Confidentiality Agreements

NDA to safeguard proprietary information.

Step 4: Get Tools & System Access

Secure login credentials and training materials will be provided.

Step 5: Review Contractor Handbook

Company values, communication etiquette, work hours, resource usage.

Step 6: Set Up Check-ins

Weekly or bi-weekly check-ins to track performance.

Step 7: Feedback System

Two-way feedback process. Timely, constructive, and actionable.
